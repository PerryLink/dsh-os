# Code of Conduct

This is a short, enforceable code, not a legal document. It applies to everyone
who participates in the project: users, contributors, reviewers, maintainers and
release managers.

## Scope

Repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, releases, and any space
where someone represents dsh-os (chat, conference talk, social post).

## Expected behavior

1. **Respect people, critique code.** Disagree with the change, not the person.
   No insults, slurs, threats, harassment, sexual attention, doxxing or
   sustained disruption.
2. **Be precise and honest.** Say what you tested, what failed, and what you do
   not know. Do not misrepresent another person's work or the project's
   affiliation with DeepSeek.
3. **Disclose conflicts of interest.** If you review, merge or publish work you
   authored, or have a commercial or financial interest in a contributor,
   vendor or package, say so in the thread and recuse from the approval or
   decision. A conflicted maintainer does not count toward the approval quorum.
4. **Respect privacy and secrets.** Never post tokens, keys, clipboard
   contents, session logs, personal data or private reports. Security issues go
   through the private channel in
   [`docs/release-policy.md`](docs/release-policy.md#10-security-response-72-hours),
   never a public issue.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, stalking, doxxing, unwanted sexual
attention, deliberate intimidation, retaliation against a reporter, publishing
private information without consent, or repeatedly disrupting discussion after
being asked to stop.

## Reporting

- For a conduct report, contact a maintainer privately through their GitHub
  profile. If the report involves that maintainer, contact a different person
  listed in [`MAINTAINERS.md`](MAINTAINERS.md). If no safe contact is
  available, use GitHub's *Report abuse* form and ask for a private channel —
  do not name people or post evidence publicly.
- Include what happened, when, where, who was involved, and links or
  screenshots if safe. You may report anonymously.
- A maintainer acknowledges every report within **72 hours** and keeps it
  confidential. The person named in the report does not investigate it and is
  recused from the decision.

## Enforcement

An uninvolved maintainer (or an external maintainer if all are involved)
investigates, hears the accused party, and decides within 14 days. Outcomes,
in order of severity:

1. private warning and a required apology or correction;
2. temporary interaction ban (7 days) from the repository spaces;
3. permanent ban from the project.

Threats, doxxing or retaliation can trigger an immediate temporary suspension
while the investigation runs. The reporter is told the outcome; the details stay
private. Either party may appeal once, within 14 days, to a different
uninvolved maintainer, who answers within 14 days.

Enforcement decisions about maintainers are recorded in the private maintainer
log; public commit access is removed when a ban is permanent.
